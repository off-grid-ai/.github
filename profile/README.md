## Pro: it works for you

One payment, one key, up to five devices.

- **Never take meeting notes again.** It records Zoom and Google Meet on your computer with no bot joining, then writes the summary with a local model.
- **Talk instead of type, anywhere.** Hold a key and your words land at your cursor in any app.
- **Find anything you've seen.** It remembers your screens, meetings, mail and docs, and you search it in plain words.
- **Know where your time went.** Reflect builds your timesheet from what you actually did, with no timers.
- **Write cleaner everywhere.** Scribe fixes grammar and rewrites to your style guide in any app, on your computer.
- **Keep notes you own.** Plain Markdown files, and it opens your Obsidian vault as it is.
- **Clear your inbox faster.** It reads your mail and calendars and drafts replies, and nothing sends without your yes.
- **Hand off the busywork.** Computer Use and Web Use do tasks in your own apps and your own signed-in browser, and you approve each one.
- **One assistant on every device.** Sync keeps chats, projects, files and your clipboard in step between phone and computer over your own network, with no cloud in between.
- **Make short films.** One sentence becomes a storyboard of shots with a saved character, narration and captions.
