## Why people switch

- **It's yours.** Models run on your device, there's no account, and your chats and files stay with you.
- **It works anywhere.** On a plane, on a locked-down network, in any country.
- **You stop paying every month.** One Pro payment covers what you'd otherwise pay several AI apps for, like meeting notes, dictation and memory.
- **You can check every claim.** The apps are open source, so you can read exactly what they do.
